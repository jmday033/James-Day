2. Confirm MDM evidence

Selections must describe work documented in this encounter. Paste exact supporting text for each element. Diagnoses on a historical problem list do not count as problems addressed.

How to classify problems and risk

Use the AMA MDM criteria. Moderate problems may include exacerbation, multiple stable chronic illnesses, or an acute systemic illness. High problems require severe exacerbation or a threat to life/bodily function. Risk concerns management decisions, not severity alone. Routine admission status, IV antibiotics, or daily labs do not automatically establish high risk.

Count a test once across ordering and reviewing; serial results of one test are not new unique tests. Identify each external source. Exclude separately reported interpretations and bundled/nonqualifying items. These entries are clinician verified, not automatically validated.

Data categories: qualifying items/history, independent interpretation, external discussion. Moderate requires one qualifying category; high requires two. Overall MDM requires two of the three elements.

3. Overnight timing

Calendar date drives the charge. A continuous service through midnight is reported on the start date with all of its time. Your group gets one hospital E/M per patient per date.

4. Time and exceptions

Separately reportable services

Each needs its own documented time that is not counted in the E/M total.

Discharge uses total discharge management time. Critical care add-on calculation is implemented only for Medicare. Prolonged service eligibility needs separate review.
