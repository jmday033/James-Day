    Information to gather

      This Quick Look does not estimate a dollar difference for medical-school entry. The attending benchmarks would give a misleading answer before training.

      Things to try

      1. Increase active years served and years planned in the Navy until the stay path reaches 20 years. Watch the pension estimate change.
      2. Switch among Doximity, Medscape, and SalaryDr. Their different survey methods move the civilian salary comparison.
      3. Change pay grade or duty ZIP to see how Navy pay and housing allowance change.
      4. Open the full model and set the real discount rate to 0%. Compare how much more future pension payments count today.
      What this Quick Look assumes

      2026 dollars; a new retention bonus is included only in the renewal view when its amount and eligibility are confirmed; 2026 basic-pay longevity steps; 2026 BAH for the entered duty ZIP, O-4/O-5/O-6, and with dependents; $328.48 monthly BAS; $43,000 annual IP and $8,000 BCP; current pay grade held throughout; 0% real civilian salary growth; 5% real discount rate. Salary sources are published estimates, not job offers.

      The headline is a gross-cash comparison adjusted for a conditional BRS pension estimate. It excludes individual income and payroll taxes, civilian employer benefits, health costs, malpractice, GI Bill transfer, and TSP contributions. The pension estimate appears only if the stay path reaches 20 eligible active years; it uses a projected final-three-year basic-pay average, a 2% BRS multiplier, 30 payment years, and 22% illustrative federal pension tax. It assumes completion of the chosen path and does not include a possible Reserve pension. Check your actual retirement system and service credit in the full model.
