Decision assumptions

This is a conditional financial comparison, not a retention prediction or an exact personal value of Navy service. BAH and BAS are already in Navy cash and are generally tax-exempt. Taxes are included only if you enter both tax estimates; the optional pension estimate is conditional on the stated assumptions. The break-even interpretation is a threshold to investigate, not a measured preference. This version evaluates the existing compensation package only.

Data and definitions

2026 O-4/O-5/O-6 basic pay: DFAS. Officer BAS: DoD. Navy FY26 primary-care IP, BCP, and four-year RB: Navy Medicine. San Diego 92134 BAH: DTMO lookup.

Marit Health San Diego published specialty benchmarks: pediatrics, internal medicine, family medicine. These are city estimates from self-reported data, not ZIP-specific or verified civilian-only offers. Methodology.

Doximity 2026 national specialty averages reflect 2025 responses. Medscape 2026 national specialty averages reflect full-time physicians' 2025 total compensation; the three displayed values are rounded to $1,000. Neither is a San Diego estimate. MGMA and AMGA require a user-provided specialty and geography cut; no values are preloaded.

10 U.S.C. § 1089 generally makes the federal remedy exclusive for military medical personnel acting within the scope of duty. Outside work is a separate coverage question. Civilian premium and tail responsibility depends on the employment contract and policy. The $0 example avoids counting employer-paid coverage or a cost already included in reported compensation.

Retirement rules: DoD. A 2012 White Coat Investor essay motivates a longer stay-or-separate career comparison; its historical dollar examples are not used here. GI Bill transfer rules: VA. Sample estimate sources: IRS 2026 brackets, SSA payroll rules, California 2025 tax schedule, California 2026 SDI, KFF premiums, BRS TSP, and IRS 401(k) compensation limit. Model is illustrative and should be checked against an actual LES, tax return, contract, and job offer before individual use.
