Decision assumptions

This is a compensation comparison, not an estimate of retention elasticity or the probability that a particular physician stays. Taxes, civilian benefits, workload, professional opportunities, moves, and service obligations may change the decision.

Data and definitions

2026 O-4 basic pay: DFAS. Officer BAS: DoD. Navy FY26 primary-care IP, BCP, and four-year RB: Navy Medicine. San Diego 92134 BAH: DTMO lookup.

Marit Health San Diego published specialty benchmarks: pediatrics, internal medicine, family medicine. These are city estimates from self-reported data, not ZIP-specific or verified civilian-only offers. Methodology.

Retirement rules: DoD. GI Bill transfer rules: VA. Model is illustrative and should be checked against an actual LES, contract, and job offer before individual use.
