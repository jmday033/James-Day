Civilian job
Starting value: 5%, a planning assumption. Someone who leaves now has years of civilian seniority, partnership, or productivity by the time a 20-year retiree starts over, and clinical skills can fade in late-career administrative jobs. 0% assumes no difference.
First-year pay: 100% is typical for employed jobs with a guaranteed starting salary; productivity-based jobs often pay less in year one. Real pay growth: 0%. Physician pay rose 2% in 2025 (Doximity 2026) while prices rose 3.4% (CPI, Aug. 2026). Cost of leaving: $2,500 for DEA registration ($888 per 3 years), a state license, and credentialing; relocation is often paid by the employer. Employer retirement: 4.6%, the average promised match (Vanguard, How America Saves 2025). Civilian family health premiums (KFF 2025 worker share) are always counted.

    Assumptions and sources

    Constant 2026 dollars; year-end cash flows. Navy pay: 2026 basic pay, BAH for the duty ZIP, BAS, FY 2026 incentive pay and four-year retention bonus for the specialty (renewed through 20, one agreement, or none), $8,000 board certification pay, BRS continuation pay if selected. Specialties without their own Navy row use the subspecialty category noted beside them; surgical subspecialties are assumed to be Category I. Civilian pay: Doximity 2026 national averages by specialty. Promotions: O-5 at 15 and O-6 at 21 years after commissioning. BAH and BAS are tax-free.

    Taxes: 2026 federal brackets, standard deduction, and Child Tax Credit; Social Security and Medicare; 2026 state wage-tax sample; California disability insurance on civilian pay in California. The pension is taxed at the rate entered.

    Pension: 2% (BRS) or 2.5% (Legacy) × 20 years × the average of the last three years of basic pay, paid for the life-table years, discounted at the pension rate. Retiree TRICARE, VA offset or concurrent receipt, and the Survivor Benefit Plan follow the settings above. The answer assumes the stay path reaches 20. After 20, the retiree's civilian pay is reduced by the percentage entered (default 5%) until the work-until age, compared with someone who left earlier. Years still under obligation are not counted, because both paths are in the Navy then.

    VA route: leave active duty for a VA staff-physician job until the work-until age, compared with the private job. VA pay (base plus market pay) starts at the private salary capped at the 2026 Tier 1 maximum for the specialty's VA pay table; it counts toward the FERS High-3. VA pay is taxed in the civilian job state without California disability insurance; the VA adds 5% TSP contributions, takes a 4.4% FERS contribution (employees hired after 2013), and covers malpractice under the Federal Tort Claims Act. Health premiums, disability insurance, and VA disability pay are treated as equal on both jobs. FERS pension: 1% (1.1% if leaving at 62 or later with 20 years) × creditable years × High-3, paid at once if leaving at 62, at 60 with 20 years, or at 57 with 30 years, otherwise from 62; at least 5 VA years are required. The buyback deposit is 3% of military basic pay, estimated from the 2026 pay table (slightly high for earlier years), paid in the first VA year. The pension is discounted at the pension rate plus 0.5% a year because FERS cost-of-living raises trail inflation. Not counted: retiree federal health coverage, the FERS supplement before 62, survivor annuities, and VA performance pay.
