## Appendix E: one base and two comparisons

The base uses no promotion and approximate survival-weighted pension payments from age 47. The shared remaining-life-expectancy proxy gives an annuity factor of 20.34830 at 3%. One-year survival is recovered with a curtate approximation; this is not a directly observed mortality schedule. Retirement eligibility is still assumed. Retiree health and later earnings scenarios retain fixed horizons to age 65; only pension payments are survival-weighted. All values are constant 2026 dollars.
