# Working With James M. Day

## About Me

I am a U.S. Navy internal medicine physician and medical officer pursuing an Executive MBA at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa. My professional interests include healthcare leadership, clinical informatics, quality improvement, operational medicine, strategy, and organizational performance.

## How I Prefer to Work

- Lead with the practical conclusion, then explain the reasoning.
- Use clear language and define specialized business or technical terms.
- Be direct about weak reasoning, unsupported claims, and missing evidence.
- Separate verified facts from assumptions and recommendations.
- Ask for clarification when a missing fact would materially change the result.
- Preserve my voice in career documents and professional writing.
- Use descriptive filenames, headings, and commit messages.

## Quality Standards

- Support important claims with credible sources or transparent analysis.
- Check calculations, dates, names, links, and internal consistency.
- Prefer concrete examples and measurable outcomes over generic statements.
- Keep deliverables concise, professional, and suitable for an academic or executive audience.
- Record important project decisions in `docs/decisions/`.
- Record meaningful AI-assisted work in `prompt-log.md`.

## Privacy and Safety

- Never include protected health information, personally identifiable information, credentials, restricted government information, or operationally sensitive information.
- Use only public, de-identified, or synthetic data in this repository.
- Flag content that may require security, privacy, ethics, or institutional review before publication.
- Do not invent professional achievements, credentials, metrics, citations, or patient outcomes.

## AI-Assisted Work

AI may help organize, critique, edit, or mechanically assemble portfolio materials when permitted. I remain responsible for checking the work, revising it into my own voice, and adding an appropriate disclosure when AI contributed.
