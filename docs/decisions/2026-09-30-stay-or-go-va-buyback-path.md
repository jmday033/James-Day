# Stay or go? adds a VA job path with FERS military buyback

The lab now compares four paths against leaving now for a private civilian job: finish 20 in the Selected Reserve, leave for a VA staff-physician job, and stay on active duty to 20. The VA path is added to the existing page rather than a separate tab because it answers the same leave-or-stay question with the same inputs.

Mechanism: a FERS employee with honorable post-1956 active duty can deposit 3% of military basic pay to add those years to FERS creditable service (OPM Service Credit). Interest is not charged if the deposit is paid within about two years of FERS hire. The deposit is not allowed for years on which Navy retired pay is drawn, so the option applies only to physicians who leave before 20.

Assumptions:

- VA pay (base plus market pay) defaults to the private salary, capped at the 2026 Tier 1 maximum for the specialty's VA pay table: $315,000 for Pay Table 1 and $400,000 for Pay Table 2. Users should enter an actual offer. Market pay counts toward High-3 (OPM); performance pay does not.
- VA employment runs from the decision year to the work-until age. VA pay is taxed in the civilian job state without California disability insurance.
- The VA adds 5% TSP agency contributions and takes a 4.4% FERS-FRAE employee contribution. Malpractice is covered by the Federal Tort Claims Act.
- Health premiums, disability insurance, VA disability pay, and the one-time cost of leaving are treated as equal on the VA and private jobs.
- FERS annuity: 1% × creditable years × High-3, or 1.1% when leaving at 62 or later with 20 years.
  - Immediate annuity at 62 (5 years), 60 (20 years), or 57 (30 years).
  - Otherwise deferred to 62. Five VA years are required to vest.
- The deposit is 3% of military basic pay, estimated from the 2026 table at the grade held each year. This slightly overstates earlier years. The deposit is paid in the first VA year.
- The pension is discounted at the pension rate plus 0.5% a year, because FERS cost-of-living adjustments trail inflation.

Not modeled:

- Retiree FEHB
- FERS supplement before 62
- FERS survivor annuity
- VA performance pay
- Combining the VA and Reserve paths

Active-duty and Reserve results are unchanged.

Example (internal medicine, 10 years, O-4, San Diego defaults): VA route about +$242k versus the private job. That total includes −$371k in salary and benefits and a $640k FERS pension ($131.7k a year from 65, 38 creditable years). Of the pension, the buyback adds about $142k net for a deposit of about $28k.
