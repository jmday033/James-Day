# Stay or go? adds a Reserve completion path

The lab now compares three paths: leave now, leave now and finish 20 qualifying years in the Selected Reserve, and stay on active duty to 20. Both service paths are valued against leaving outright, in today's dollars after taxes.

Reserve assumptions: active years earn 365 points each; each Reserve year earns 77 points by default (15 membership, 48 drills, 14 days of annual training). Retired pay is points ÷ 360 × 2.5% (Legacy) or 2.0% (BRS) × High-3, paid from age 60 unless the user enters an earlier start earned through qualifying mobilization. High-3 reuses the active path's final-years basic pay in today's dollars, a simplification of the non-regular retired-pay rules. Retiree TRICARE is valued only from the pension start to 65, because TRICARE Retired Reserve before 60 costs its full premium. Drill pay is taxed at the user's marginal rate, and 10 civilian workdays per year are subtracted for annual training.

Not modeled: Reserve special and incentive pays, mobilization and its pay, RCSBP, and VA offset. These limits are stated on the page. Active-path calculations are unchanged.
