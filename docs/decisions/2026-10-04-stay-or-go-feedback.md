# Stay or go? reader feedback

The author requested a public feedback form with email delivery to his supplied address. A FormSubmit POST endpoint supports the static site, with default CAPTCHA and a honeypot. Feedback is required; name and reply email are optional. Calculator values are excluded. The privacy statement distinguishes calculator inputs from submitted feedback, and feedback controls do not trigger calculator updates.

Delivery requires recipient activation through FormSubmit’s first-use confirmation email. No financial engine changes.

Validation: HTML parsing checked unique IDs, endpoint, required/optional fields, and the submission field allowlist; JavaScript syntax checked. Delivery remains unverified pending activation.
