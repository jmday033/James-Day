# Stay or go? starts from a blank slate

At the author's request, after reader feedback that the page looked built for one case (a 10-year O-4 internist in San Diego), Stay or go? version 2.0 no longer pre-fills that case.

**What changed.** Six questions now start empty and are required before any answer appears: active years served, specialty, current rank, family, Navy duty-station ZIP, and civilian job state. ZIP and civilian state moved out of the optional Refine section because they set housing allowance and state tax. Until all six are filled, the answer box lists what is still needed and the result panels stay hidden. The San Diego internist remains available only as an optional example button, alongside the existing family-medicine and pediatrics examples and a new non-primary-care example (O-5 anesthesiologist, 12 years, Portsmouth, Virginia). The page's specialty list (48 specialties, Doximity 2026 civilian averages, FY 2026 Navy special pays) and BAH lookup for any duty-station ZIP were already in place; the blank start makes that scope visible.

**Discount-rate wording.** The pay discount-rate help text now says why it is higher than the pension rate: future salaries, especially civilian ones, are less certain than an inflation-adjusted, federally backed pension. Rates are unchanged (5% real for pay, 3% real for pension).

**Unchanged.** The calculation engine, its defaults, and all tests; the advanced lab; the GMO and medical-school views; and the paper.
