## Journal format checked during this session

[Military Medicine author instructions](https://academic.oup.com/milmed/pages/General_Instructions): commentary body up to 2,500 words, generally at most 15 references and 2 tables/figures, unstructured abstract 250–500 words; case report body up to 2,000 words, abstract up to 250 words, up to 4 tables/figures and 40 references, CARE guidance and informed consent. Recheck before submission.
