# Stage 1 hypothesis

I expect it will be cheaper to continue using Uber for my as-needed rides than to purchase a used car.

Uber costs occur only when I ride, while a car creates costs while idle: depreciation, insurance, registration, maintenance, and the opportunity cost of cash. Because Uber costs $50 one way, the hypothesis should fail above some recurring frequency.

## Prediction

Uber will remain cheaper below eight one-way rides per month (about four round trips). The $5,000 car should cross first.

## Falsification rule

Reject the hypothesis for a car when its all-in five-year cost is below Uber's cost for the same number of rides.
