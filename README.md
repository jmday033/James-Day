James “Mitch” Day is a U.S. Navy internal medicine physician and medical officer with experience spanning clinical medicine, operational medicine, healthcare leadership, and clinical informatics. His career has taken him from residency training in San Diego to leadership roles in Guam and Hawaii, where he has cared for patients while working to improve the systems that support them.

His primary interests include healthcare leadership, clinical informatics, quality improvement, and the intersection of medicine and organizational strategy. In the Navy, he has led clinical teams and initiatives focused on improving access to care, virtual medicine, medical readiness, and healthcare workflows. These experiences have reinforced his belief that excellent healthcare depends not only on sound clinical decisions, but also on effective teams, technology, and organizational leadership.

He is currently pursuing an Executive MBA at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa’s Shidler College of Business to strengthen his skills in strategy, finance, and management. His goal is to combine those skills with his experience as a physician and military officer to lead meaningful change in healthcare. Ultimately, he hopes to help build healthcare systems that work better for both the patients who depend on them and the people who deliver their care.
