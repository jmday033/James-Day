# James “Mitch” Day

James “Mitch” Day is a U.S. Navy internal medicine physician and medical officer with experience spanning clinical medicine, operational medicine, healthcare leadership, and clinical informatics. He has served in clinical and leadership roles in San Diego, Guam, and Hawaiʻi, caring for patients while improving the systems that support them.

His interests include healthcare leadership, clinical informatics, quality improvement, and the intersection of medicine and organizational strategy. He is currently pursuing an Executive MBA at the University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa’s Shidler College of Business to strengthen his skills in strategy, finance, and management. His goal is to combine those skills with his experience as a physician and military officer to lead meaningful change in healthcare.
